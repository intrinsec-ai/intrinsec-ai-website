Why a baseline

The list of things that go wrong keeps growing.

Attacks and ordinary breakage look the same from the outside. Both are deviations from how your workload normally computes.

  • Prompt injection
  • Jailbreaks
  • Sensitive-data exposure
  • Rogue tool calls
  • Broken tool calls
  • Hallucination
  • Drift and off-domain use
  • Multimodal (image, audio, video)

How it works

Baseline. Surface. Tag.

  1. 01 · Baseline

    Learn how your model fires.

    We watch the model behind your agent during normal traffic and build a baseline of normal for your workload, learned from your own traffic rather than a generic attack list.

  2. 02 · Surface

    Score every request against it.

    Anything atypical stands out, whatever the source or modality, even when the input itself reads perfectly fine.

  3. 03 · Tag

    Label what surfaced.

    Domain, task, disposition, safety flags — already labeled when they reach your alerting stack.

Off the request path. vLLM, SGLang, and inference gateways. On-prem or cloud.

Where this is heading

The gateway keeps moving toward the model.

  1. 2009 – 2023

  2. 2023 – 2026

    AI gateway

    Governs prompts and tokens. One API, spend control, guardrail hooks.

  3. Next
